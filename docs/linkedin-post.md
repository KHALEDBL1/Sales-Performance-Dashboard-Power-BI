I’m excited to share my completed academic portfolio project: **Sales Performance Dashboard in Power BI**.

I built this project to transform raw AdventureWorks CSV files into a clear, executive-style view of sales performance, revenue, and profitability.

Key results:

• 84,174 units sold  
• $24.91M in total revenue  
• $10.46M in net profit  
• Q2 was the strongest quarter for profitability  
• Bikes generated 94.9% of total revenue

The project covered the complete analytics workflow: data import and preparation in Power Query, data modeling, DAX measures, KPI validation, dashboard design, and data storytelling.

Tools and skills: Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Data Modeling, Data Visualization, and Business Intelligence.
